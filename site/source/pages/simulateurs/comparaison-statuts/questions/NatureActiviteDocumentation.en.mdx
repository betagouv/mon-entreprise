# Nature of main activity

Your type of activity will determine a large part of the calculation for social security contributions, taxes and the benefits and rights you are entitled to.

## Artisanal

To become a craftsman, you must meet the following 3 criteria:
- Your activity must be on the official list of craft trades or the official list of arts and crafts trades.
- Your company must have fewer than 11 employees when it is set up.
- You must have specific know-how and prove it with a diploma (CAP, BEP, etc.) or professional qualification.

## Commercial

Your activity is commercial if you are in one of the following situations:
- You buy goods or merchandise for resale with a view to making a profit: making money, making a profit, making a profit.
- You sell services in the hotel, restaurant, transport, entertainment, security, IT, design and other sectors.
- You rent out goods, merchandise or services

## Liberal

A liberal activity consists of services of a mainly intellectual nature.

You render a paid service.

You must have a diploma or a professional qualification in the field.

## Multiple activities

In the case of multiple activities, the main activity is deemed to be the oldest.

However, from the third calendar year following that in which the pluriactivity situation began, interested parties may request that the activity that generated the highest amount of sales or revenue excluding taxes over the last three years be considered their main activity.
