# Regulated or unregulated liberal profession

## Regulated

Certain liberal professions are legally classified as such, and their
professional titles are protected by law. Members of these professions
must comply with strict ethical rules and are subject to the supervision
of their professional bodies (professional association, chamber or union).

> Examples of regulated professions: architect, lawyer, nurse, doctor…

## Unregulated

This refers to other individuals who practise a science or an art in which
intellectual activity plays the primary role. Their income must constitute
remuneration for personal work performed independently, without an
employer-employee relationship, while making them accountable for their
technical and ethical responsibilities.

> Examples of unregulated professions: software developer, historian, urban
> planner…
