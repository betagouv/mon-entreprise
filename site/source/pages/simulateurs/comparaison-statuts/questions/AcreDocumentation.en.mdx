# Acre

The assistance for business creation or takeover (Acre) scheme provides a
**partial exemption** from social security contributions for 12 months, known
as the start-up exemption.

To qualify, you must **submit an application to Urssaf** within 60 days of
setting up your business.

Before 2026, the Acre exemption was granted automatically and was available
to all individuals starting a business.

## Eligibility criteria

You can benefit from the Acre scheme only if you fall into **one of the following
categories** when setting up your business:
- Jobseeker receiving unemployment benefits;
- Jobseeker not receiving unemployment benefits but registered with France Travail
for at least 6 months during the previous 18 months;
- Recipient of the Active Solidarity Income (RSA) or the Specific Solidarity
Allowance (ASS);
- Person aged 18 to 25 inclusive;
- Person under 30 who is not receiving unemployment benefits because they have
not worked long enough to qualify for them;
- Person under 30 who has been officially recognised as having a disability;
- Employee or former employee of a company undergoing court-supervised
safeguarding, receivership or liquidation proceedings who takes over that
company's business;
- Person who has entered into a Business Project Support Contract (Contrat
d'appui au projet d'entreprise, or Cape), provided they meet one of the seven
eligibility criteria listed above on the date the contract is signed;
- Person setting up a business located in a Priority Urban Policy Area (Quartier
prioritaire de la politique de la ville, or QPV);
- Recipient of the Shared Child-Rearing Benefit (Prestation partagée d'éducation
de l'enfant, or PreParE);
- Person carrying out their business activity in a France Rural Revitalisation
Zone (Zone France ruralités revitalisation, or ZFRR) or a France Rural
Revitalisation Zone Plus (Zone France ruralités revitalisation « plus », or ZFRR+).

In addition, you must not have benefited from the Acre scheme during the previous
three years.

## For companies

For **companies**, you must meet at least one of the following conditions:
- You and your family members hold more than 50% of the company's share capital,
of which at least 35% is held by you personally;
- You manage the company and you and your family members hold at least one-third
of the share capital, of which at least 25% is held by you personally, provided
that no other person holds more than 50% of the share capital;
- You hold, together with the other individuals applying for the Acre scheme,
more than 50% of the share capital, provided that at least one of these
individuals is a company director and each applicant holds a share of the
capital representing at least 10% of the share held by the largest shareholder.
